# Editable manual and imported reviews

- Every new Manual Add requires a provenance reason.
- Manual and imported historical reviews get an **Edit import** button in Review Manager.
- Old imports with no reason display **Import reason not recorded** until edited.
- The editor can update reviewer/date/stars/headline/body/verified status.
- AI title generation is available for historical imports too.
- Sourness, Sweetness and Flavour are independently opt-in and can be removed from old imports.
- Normal storefront/email reviews remain non-editable through this historical-import editor.
