# Module guide

## Reviews

Folder: `src/modules/reviews`

Responsible for:

- Public review submission.
- Approved review listing.
- Bulk imports.
- One-use signed review tokens.
- Duplicate order/product/customer blocking.
- Manual moderation.
- Widget compatibility endpoints.

## Loyalty

Folder: `src/modules/loyalty`

Responsible for:

- Private hashed loyalty ledger.
- Manual points.
- Summary metrics.

## Discounts

Folder: `src/modules/discounts`

Responsible for:

- Discount rules.
- Reward types.
- Product targeting foundation.
- Integration with core Shopify product search.

## Cart Rewards

Folder: `src/modules/cart-rewards`

Responsible for:

- Tiered cart reward campaigns.
- Reward appearance configuration.
- Active campaigns for storefront/cart drawer widgets.
- Monthly campaign view.

## Campaigns

Folder: `src/modules/campaigns`

Responsible for:

- Calendar planning across modules.
- Campaign objectives.
- Campaign metrics foundation.

## Help Centre

Folder: `src/modules/help`

Responsible for:

- Deterministic help articles.
- Setup checklist.
- Phase-one support drawer foundation.
