Documentation

Review Manager

Manage every review from one place. Reviews can be accepted, held, rejected, trashed, restored, or replied to.

  • Public reply: shown below the review on the storefront.
  • Private note: internal only and never returned by public APIs.
  • Trash: hides reviews immediately and keeps them until the configured retention period expires.
Approval rules

Auto-approval only runs after the review has passed source and verification checks. Test reviews cannot be published, even manually.

  • Verified-purchase auto-approval only accepts signed request, import, or manual verification.
  • Unsigned storefront reviews stay unverified.
  • Low-star reviews remain pending if below your threshold.
Conditional sliders

Conditional sliders appear when a product matches a tag or metafield rule. Scores are optional and are only saved when the customer moves the slider.

Email Builder

  • Test sends are tracked as test campaigns.
  • Open and click tracking are added automatically to test emails.
  • Shopify Flow HTML is collapsible because it is only needed when pasting into Flow.

Global carousel

Verified purchase logic
Verified is now server-controlled. Public requests cannot set themselves as verified.
  • Storefront modal: always starts unverified.
  • Signed review-request link: verified only when the token signature, shop, email, order, product, and expiry pass.
  • Imported reviews: can be marked verified by the merchant and are labelled as imported.
  • Manual admin verification: sets a manual verification note.
  • Test links: are never verified and never public.
Review-link expiry and one-time use

Signed review tokens include an expiry and are marked as used after the first successful submission. Reuse returns an error.

Auto-approval

Auto-approval checks rating, test status, deletion status, and verification status. If “Verified Purchases Only” is selected, unsigned reviews stay pending.

Discounts

Reserved for review-linked offers, discount rules, and Shopify Function-backed incentives.

Loyalty

The Loyalty beta stores rewards using a shop-scoped hashed customer reference only. It supports review-triggered discount templates and points rules with delays, verified-only controls, and minimum-star conditions.

  • Discount templates: percentage or future fixed-value thank-you rewards after review submission or approval.
  • Points rules: award points after conditions such as approved verified review after 28 days.
  • Privacy: loyalty ledger rows do not store customer names, emails, addresses, or phone numbers.
